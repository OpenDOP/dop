# ADR-NNN: Short title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Draft | Superseded by ADR-NNN | Rejected
- **Date:** DD.MM.YYYY
- **Deciders:** @merttemiz, @hasan4adnan

## Context

What forces the decision. The problem, the constraints that are not negotiable, and what
breaks if the decision is postponed. State facts and where they come from; if something is
unmeasured, say so.

## Decision

What is decided, in the present tense and in one or two paragraphs. Specific enough that a
reader can tell whether a given pull request obeys it.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not |
|:------------|:--------|
| | |

Record the alternatives that were seriously weighed, so they are not re-proposed.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, what it commits the project to, and what has to be
revisited later and when. Include the costs, not only the benefits.
